NILE CALLIGRAPHY March 2024 • Kampala, Uganda

The First Exhibition

A three-day presentation of handcrafted Islamic calligraphy, heritage and artistic expression, bringing Nile Calligraphy's work into a public cultural setting.

Location Eureka Place
City Ntinda, Kampala
Duration Friday–Sunday
First Exhibition March 2024
Beyond The Studio

Where the artwork met the public.

Nile Calligraphy's first exhibition created an opportunity for visitors to encounter the artwork in person, ask questions about the pieces and engage directly with the artist.

The exhibition brought together Islamic calligraphy, handcrafted artwork and elements of Ugandan heritage in a setting designed around conversation, craftsmanship and cultural connection.

The Experience

More than a display of paintings.

Visitors were able to speak directly with the artist and ask about the stories, techniques and ideas behind the works.

Questions ranged from landscapes and horse paintings to the stories behind individual pieces, turning the exhibition into a space for conversation as well as viewing.

VISITOR EXPERIENCE

Art became conversation.

Visitors did not simply walk past the paintings. They could meet the artist, ask questions and hear the stories behind the work.

Questions From Visitors

What people wanted to know.

What inspired the landscapes?
