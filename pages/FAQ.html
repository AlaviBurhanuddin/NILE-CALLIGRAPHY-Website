Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Before You Order?

Learn more about our original Islamic calligraphy, commissions, ordering process, delivery and exhibition work.

Original Paintings

Are your paintings original artworks?

Yes. Our original paintings are individually handcrafted Islamic calligraphy artworks created as distinctive physical pieces.

Can I see the available paintings before ordering?

Yes. Our Original Paintings catalogue presents the available collection with detailed information about each artwork. For the latest availability, you can also contact us directly through WhatsApp.

Are the paintings suitable for homes and offices?

Yes. Our artworks are created for meaningful spaces including homes, offices, institutions, mosques and other environments where Islamic art can be appreciated.

Can I ask about the story behind a particular artwork?

Yes. We believe the meaning and thought behind an artwork are an important part of the experience. Our catalogue will provide deeper information about each piece, and you can contact us for additional questions.

Custom Commissions

Do you accept custom artwork commissions?

Yes. We accept selected custom commissions depending on the artwork, requirements and project scope.

Can I request a specific Quranic verse or Arabic phrase?

Custom requests can be discussed with our team. Contact us with the verse, phrase or concept you have in mind so we can discuss the artistic possibilities.

Can you create artwork for a mosque or institution?

Yes. We are open to larger projects for mosques, schools, hospitals, hotels, offices and other institutions. Larger projects can be discussed directly with our team.

Ordering & Enquiries

How do I purchase an artwork?

Browse the artwork catalogue and select the piece you are interested in. For availability, pricing confirmation and purchase arrangements, contact us through WhatsApp.

Can I ask questions before deciding to buy?

Absolutely. You can contact us with questions about an artwork, its availability, customization, delivery or any other purchase-related matter before making a decision.

How can I contact Nile Calligraphy?

The fastest way to reach us for artwork enquiries is through WhatsApp. You can also use the Contact page for additional contact information.

Delivery

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery arrangements depend on the artwork, destination and order. Contact us with your location and artwork requirements so we can discuss the available options.

Can artwork be delivered outside Kampala?

Delivery outside Kampala can be discussed depending on the artwork and destination. Contact us before ordering so the appropriate arrangement can be confirmed.

Exhibitions & Partnerships

Do you hold Islamic art exhibitions?

Yes. Exhibitions are an important part of Nile Calligraphy's vision for presenting Islamic art and connecting original artworks with collectors and the wider community.

Can organisations partner with Nile Calligraphy?

Yes. We are open to suitable partnerships, community projects, institutional commissions and exhibition collaborations.

Still Have a Question?

If you cannot find the answer you're looking for, speak directly with the Nile Calligraphy team. We can help you with artwork enquiries, commissions, availability and other questions.
